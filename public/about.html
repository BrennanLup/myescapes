My Escapes

Every escape room you've played, pinned up as a polaroid in your own cozy 3D room.

Open My Escapes

What it does

Your own room

Sign in with Google and get a personal 3D room. Each escape room you log becomes a polaroid on your board, with your scores and notes.

Log by voice

On your phone, record a quick voice note after a game. It's transcribed and turned into a draft review, which you check before saving.

Find rooms

A public directory of escape room venues and games, with reviews from players. Your reviews are public by default, and you can make each one friends-only or private.

Play with friends

Tag the friends you played with. A shared visit is logged once, and everyone keeps their own review.

Escape of the Day

A small daily puzzle, the same for everyone, with a share grid and a streak.

Signing in

My Escapes uses Google sign-in and only asks for your name, email address and profile picture. We don't get access to your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account. Read the privacy policy to see what we store and how to delete it.
