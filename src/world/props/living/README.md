# Living props (Furniture Set A)

`LivingProps` mounts the cozy living corner and room dressing. Everything is generated in code (no downloaded models): soft deformable rounded boxes, lathe-turned profiles, tubes and parametric surfaces with smooth normals (see `geo.ts`):

- **Living area** (`ANCHORS.livingArea`): layered woven rug with stripes, diamonds and curled fringe tassels; a plum couch facing west (−X) with an upholstered base and back frame, puffed arms, crowned seat cushions that sag a little, reclined back cushions, welted piping (tubes) along the cushion edges, tapered splayed walnut legs, plump pinched-corner pillows and a thick knitted throw draped over the arm with folds, stripes and tassels; orange and teal bean bags (one 56x40 sphere each, pushed into a slouchy shape with a backrest, seat dent and creases); a knitted pouf (lathe with cable-knit ridges); a coffee table with a chamfered rounded top, aprons, lower shelf and tapered brass-capped legs, a hollow mug with handle and cocoa, board games with turned pawns and a die; a turned side table with hardback books (covers + page blocks) and a succulent rosette.
- **Bookshelf** (`ANCHORS.shelves`, east wall): a 3-bay, 4-level unit with bevelled boards, front lips, an overhanging top, a tongue-and-groove back and turned bun feet; about 130 books (instanced covers with a rounded spine and darker bands, cream page blocks; varied sizes, some pushed back or tipped, a few leaning, flat stacks), vinyl sleeves, a record player (platter, grooved record, S-shaped tonearm with counterweight, open smoked dust cover) with speakers (woofer cones, tweeter domes), a globe on a turned stand, a puzzle box, a turned trophy cup, a ribbed cactus, a framed crew photo under glass, woven baskets with rope rims, and a pothos whose vines spill over the top.
- **Lamps**: a floor lamp behind the couch's north arm (kept off the room's z = 0 centre line so the walk east from the centre is clear) with a turned weighted base, knuckled brass pole, spider and a cord across the floor; its drum shade (real wall thickness, brass inner/outer rims) is centred exactly on `LAMPS.floorLamp`. A ribbed paper-lantern pendant at `LAMPS.pendantA` and a cone pendant (thick shade, inner rim, bulb) at `LAMPS.pendantB`, both with a turned ceiling canopy, cord grip and cord. Shades and bulbs use `emissiveWarm` and never cast shadows, so the lighting workstream's lights can sit inside them.
- **Plants**: monsteras (curved stems, leaves built as fans of lobes that split apart towards the edge, so no alpha), snake plants (V-folded blades that twist and bend) and leafy bushes (70 cupped leaves over a soft core) in free corners and spots (see `FLOOR_PLANTS` in `layout.ts`), all in turned pots with lips and soil; plus three hanging pothos bowls on cords (near the west window, near the south-east window, and above the corridor between the floor lamp and the wishlist) with curving tube vines and heart-shaped leaves.

## Files
- `layout.ts`: world-space positions and the `LIVING_COLLIDERS` list (pure data). Change placements here.
- `geo.ts`: the geometry kit: `softBox` (rounded box that can be deformed, e.g. with `cushion({ crown, sag, bulge, pinch, wrinkle })`), `lathe`, `tube`, `helix`, `surface` (parametric sheet, optionally thickened), `slab` (chamfered board via ExtrudeGeometry), `deform`, `smoothNormals` (seam-free, area-weighted) and `waves` (cheap seeded noise).
- `builder.ts`: `PartsBuilder<M>` transforms parts into world space and merges them into one geometry per (material, castShadow) bucket. It has `box`/`cyl`/`sphere`/`torus`/`rod` plus `soft`, `lathe`, `tube` and `slab`. It is generic over the material key so the desk reuses it.
- `furniture.ts`, `shelf.ts`, `plants.ts`: the generators. `set.ts` assembles everything.
- `living.test.ts`: checks that colliders sit inside the room, keep 1.5 m paths from spawn to the board, desk, wishlist and door clear, stay off other anchors' footprints and don't overlap each other. It also checks lamp shade positions, the draw-call and triangle budgets, that geometry is finite with unit normals and UVs, that emissive parts never cast shadows and that the main furniture does.

## Performance
32 merged meshes plus 3 `InstancedMesh`es (129 book covers, their page blocks, 9 record sleeves): 35 draw calls, under the 60 draw-call target. About 147k triangles (130k merged + 18k instanced), tested to stay under 200k. Shadow casting is per bucket: furniture, pots and leaves cast; shades, bulbs, piping, cords, rug layers and tiny trinkets don't. Geometry is built once in `useMemo` (about 0.3 s in Node) and disposed on unmount.

## Colliders
`living:couch`, `living:beanbag-orange`, `living:beanbag-teal`, `living:pouf`, `living:coffee-table`, `living:side-table`, `living:floor-lamp`, `living:bookshelf`, `living:plant-*`. The rug and the hanging plants (above head height) have none.

## Simplifications
- `leafDark`, `vinyl` and the white book base are local clones of the shared `leaf`, `metalDark` and `paper` materials with a new colour, because the contract has no ids for them.
- These are decorative props only, not interactables.
- The shelf is centred on the anchor depth-wise, so its back sits about 3 cm from the east wall. It stands on 10 cm feet, so the under-shelf LED strip shows beneath it.
